explanation: "Proxy testing uses practical substitute measurements when direct real-world testing is not yet feasible. The goal is to answer specific performance questions with the simplest credible method under project constraints [1], [6]."
evidence: |
  In Praxis I, the team used proxy tests to compare concept performance under first-year constraints: writing speed versus a control condition, smudging through color-difference comparison, wrist-angle ergonomics using a modified RULA-style check, and concept mass checks. As shown in Figure 7, these proxy methods converted broad concept claims into directly comparable evidence across roller support, dummy marker, platform, and air-cushion concepts [6], [7].

  ![Praxis I proxy-testing evidence](/images/projects/praxis-i/figure-7.png)

utilityAssessment: "Proxy testing made convergence evidence-based instead of preference-based. It exposed tradeoffs clearly and helped justify why the roller concept was the strongest overall option rather than just the most intuitive one [6]."
