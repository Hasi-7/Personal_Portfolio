name: "Proxy Testing"
slug: "proxy-testing"
strand: "converge"
projects:
  - praxis-i
explanation: "Proxy testing uses practical substitute measurements when direct real-world testing is not yet feasible. The goal is to answer specific performance questions with the simplest credible method under project constraints."
evidence: "In Praxis I, the team used proxy tests to compare concept performance under first-year constraints: writing speed versus a control condition, smudging through color-difference comparison, wrist-angle ergonomics using a modified RULA-style check, and concept mass checks. These tests enabled direct comparison between roller support, dummy marker, platform, and air-cushion concepts."
utilityAssessment: "Proxy testing made convergence evidence-based instead of preference-based. It exposed tradeoffs clearly and helped justify why the roller concept was the strongest overall option rather than just the most intuitive one."
fitAssessment: "This fits my design practice because it balances rigor with feasibility. Praxis I reinforced that when assumptions and limits are stated clearly, proxy tests can support strong engineering decisions early in the process."
