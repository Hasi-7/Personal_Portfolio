explanation: "Stakeholder mapping is a systematic process for identifying all parties affected by or involved in a design challenge, then visualising their relationships, interests, influence, and potential conflicts. Common representations include influence-interest matrices and network maps [1], [2]."
evidence: "In Praxis I, stakeholder mapping helped us separate primary users (left-handed board writers) from secondary and tertiary stakeholders such as audience readers and facilities staff, which clarified what counted as success beyond just user comfort [6]. In Praxis II, we expanded this process for the FPV context by mapping pilots, engineers, operations/compliance, production clients, bystanders, regulators, and insurers, then explicitly identifying conflicts between speed, safety, and accountability before converging on a scoped solution [10], [14]."
utilityAssessment: "Highly useful as a starting point for any design project. It forces you to look beyond the obvious 'user' and consider the full ecosystem a design operates within. The structured visualisation makes implicit assumptions explicit, which is especially valuable in team settings where different members may have different mental models of who matters [10], [14]."
fitAssessment: "This fits how I work because I usually break complex problems down before trying to integrate everything. Stakeholder mapping helps me identify which users and constraints need to be handled first, so I can prioritize decisions properly and isolate issues instead of treating everything as equally urgent [10], [14]."
