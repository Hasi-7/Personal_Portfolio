explanation: "Stakeholder mapping is a systematic process for identifying all parties affected by or involved in a design challenge, then visualising their relationships, interests, influence, and potential conflicts. Common representations include influence-interest matrices, onion diagrams, and network maps."
evidence: "In Praxis I, stakeholder mapping helped us separate primary users (left-handed board writers) from secondary and tertiary stakeholders such as audience readers and facilities staff, which clarified what counted as success beyond just user comfort. In Praxis II, we expanded this process for the FPV context by mapping pilots, engineers, operations/compliance, production clients, bystanders, regulators, and insurers, then explicitly identifying conflicts between speed, safety, and accountability before converging on a scoped solution."
utilityAssessment: "Highly useful as a starting point for any design project. It forces you to look beyond the obvious 'user' and consider the full ecosystem a design operates within. The structured visualisation makes implicit assumptions explicit, which is especially valuable in team settings where different members may have different mental models of who matters."
fitAssessment: "Strong fit with my design practice. I find that my best design decisions come from deeply understanding context before generating solutions, and stakeholder mapping is one of the most effective tools for building that understanding. I plan to use this as a standard first step in future projects."
