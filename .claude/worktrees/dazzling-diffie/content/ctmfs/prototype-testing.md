explanation: "Prototype testing involves creating physical or digital representations of a design at varying fidelity levels and systematically evaluating them against requirements or with users. The purpose is to answer specific questions about the design before committing to a final implementation."
evidence: "In CIV102, prototype testing was literal: we tested our bridge under controlled loading to validate our structural analysis predictions. In Praxis II, our testing was more iterative — we built three prototype versions of increasing fidelity, testing each with representative users and incorporating feedback between iterations."
utilityAssessment: "Indispensable. Prototype testing is where assumptions meet reality. No amount of analysis can fully predict how a design will perform in context. The key is testing early and cheaply — a rough prototype that reveals a fundamental flaw saves weeks of wasted refinement."
fitAssessment: "Strong fit, and an area where I've grown significantly. I initially tended to over-refine before testing, wanting the prototype to be 'good enough' before showing it to anyone. I've learned that lower-fidelity tests earlier in the process are almost always more valuable than higher-fidelity tests later."
