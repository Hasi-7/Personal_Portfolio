explanation: "A design brief is a concise document that defines the scope, objectives, constraints, and success criteria for a design project. It serves as a reference point throughout the design process, helping teams stay aligned on what they're trying to achieve and why. A good design brief balances specificity with flexibility."
evidence: "For the CIV102 bridge project, I developed a design brief that translated project specifications into engineering requirements — load capacity, span, material constraints, and performance metrics. In Praxis II, our design brief was more nuanced, incorporating stakeholder needs, user requirements, and explicit trade-off priorities that guided our convergence decisions."
utilityAssessment: "Essential for any non-trivial design project. The act of writing a design brief forces you to confront ambiguity early, when it's cheapest to resolve. It also provides an objective reference point for evaluating concepts later — instead of asking 'do I like this?', you can ask 'does this meet the brief?'"
fitAssessment: "Strong fit. I've found that the quality of a design brief directly predicts the quality of the final design. Projects where I invested time in a thorough brief consistently produced better outcomes than those where I jumped straight to ideation."
