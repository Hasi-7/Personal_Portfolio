I am a first year Engineering Science student at the University of Toronto, with a passion for engineering design and curiosity across several disciplines. This portfolio is a way for me to document my engineering journey, share the projects I have worked on, reflect on the design process, and demonstrate how I think about engineering challenges.

## What I'm Working On

My current focus is to build strong fundamentals in core engineering concepts while attempting to explore my interests, in hopes that I can find my passion. Despite being pulled towards Machine Intelligence and Robotics, I want to give myself the opportunity to explore other disciplines.

Outside the classroom, I'm interested in projects that integrate hardware and software, such as my participation with U of T Formula Racing (UTFR) within the driverless division, my Praxis II project which involved drone diagnostics and more. I am greatly invested in these types of projects because I believe some of the most interesting engineering challenges emerge when you have to make physical and digital systems work together.

## Beyond Engineering

When I'm not studying or working on projects, you can find me exploring new technologies, diving into whatever rabbit hole has captured my attention that week, or even just enjoying some games to take my mind off work.
