Need: To store papers efficiently and easy access
Goals:
   -​ Papers should not fall out
          -​ Be able to store an x amount of papers without the binder rings popping open
   -​ Papers should not tear
          -​ Paper should not experience more than 1 kg of force where the rings are in
              contact with the paper
          -​ Paper should not get caught in the zipper of the binder
   -​ Environmental protection of paper
          -​ Sealed binder with an ip rating of ipx6
   -​ Easy to insert and extract papers
          -​ Have a higher minimum force to open the binder ring to prevent accidental
              “pops”
Reference Designs:
   -​ Clicking button to open and close the binder ring instead of applying force on the rings
       themselves
   -​ Use of waterproof material on the outside of the binder and sealed off by a zipper
   -​ Instead of round rings, use a flat edge ring to be in contact with the paper to increase the
       contact surface area and distribute the force experienced by the paper over a larger area
   -​ Set a clear point for which the user can know that their binder is now full, max line or
       something like that
